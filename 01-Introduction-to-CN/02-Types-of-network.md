# Types of Network

Computer networks can be classified based on their geographical coverage and size.

## 1. LAN (Local Area Network)

A **LAN** is a network that covers a small geographical area such as a room, building, office, school, or college.

### Example
- Computer lab
- Home Wi-Fi network
- Office network

### Key Points
- Small geographical area
- High speed
- Usually privately owned
- Easier to manage

---

## 2. MAN (Metropolitan Area Network)

A **MAN** is a network that covers a larger area than a LAN, usually a city or metropolitan area.

### Example
- Network connecting different branches of an organization across a city
- City-wide network

### Key Points
- Larger than LAN
- Smaller than WAN
- Covers a city or metropolitan area
- Can connect multiple LANs

---

## 3. WAN (Wide Area Network)

A **WAN** is a network that covers a very large geographical area such as countries or continents.

### Example
- The Internet
- Network connecting branches of a company across different countries

### Key Points
- Very large geographical coverage
- Connects multiple LANs and MANs
- Usually more complex than LAN
- Communication may involve leased lines, fiber, satellite, etc.

---

## Comparison

| Feature | LAN | MAN | WAN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full Form | Local Area Network | Metropolitan Area Network | Wide Area Network |
| Coverage | Small area | City / metropolitan area | Large geographical area |
| Example | Home, Office | City-wide network | Internet |
| Speed | Generally high | Generally lower than LAN | Varies |
| Ownership | Usually private | Private/Public | Often uses multiple networks/providers |

## Interview Answer

**Q: What is the difference between LAN, MAN, and WAN?**

**Answer:**

LAN covers a small geographical area such as a home, office, or building.
MAN covers a city or metropolitan area, while WAN covers a large geographical
area such as countries or continents.
